## Confirmed recovery blockers

1. **Scene clearance is not bound to the consumed source.**
   `CameraRecovery.observe_scene(received_at, has_can)` accepts no camera,
   session, or geometry identity. VisionService calls it for every fixed camera.
   After the source target becomes LOST/stale, another camera's empty frames
   can clear the consumed scene latch. A newly identified target can launch
   another physical route for the same scene, contrary to the recovery contract.

   Offline reproduction used a temporary SQLite journal, wildcard binding,
   `candidate()` from the recovery test helper, injected clock, and an injected
   async executor returning `RETURNED_AND_RELEASED`. At time 1000, launch the
   candidate and drain; update it as LOST; submit empty frames at each integer
   time 1002 through 1007; submit a fresh candidate with changed object/session
   ID at time 1007 and drain. Observed `consumed=0` before the second update and
   **two injected executor calls**. No real child process was launched.

   Owner action: persist the consumed source identity with the latch, require
   matching continuous clearance evidence, and fail closed through stream loss,
   unrelated cameras, changed epochs, and restarts. Add those regressions.

2. **Windows cancellation does not interrupt the route child.**
   `_execute` and `close` use `process.send_signal(signal.SIGINT)`. Windows
   asyncio subprocesses reject SIGINT. A harmless Python child sleeping for two
   seconds reproduced `ValueError: Unsupported signal: 2`; its returncode was
   still None. The reproduction explicitly killed and awaited the child during
   cleanup. The recovery code also needs to retain ownership until child exit
   is confirmed; recording UNKNOWN alone does not stop a running child.

   Owner action: implement platform-compatible interruption with the required
   process creation configuration, bounded exit handling, and confirmed cleanup.
   Verify using a fake child, including cancellation during stale evidence and
   controller shutdown. Preserve UNKNOWN and require reconciliation when the
   physical outcome cannot be established.
